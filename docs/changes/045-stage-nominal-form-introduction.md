# Stage Nominal Form Introduction

## Problem

Mastering an ordinary base word makes every generated nominal form eligible at
once, creating a much larger initial workload than staged verb forms.

## Desired behavior

Introduce nominal forms in useful case groups, advancing after two mastered
cards while retaining access to previously practised later forms.

## Scope

- Stage ordinary nominal and plural-pronoun profiles separately.
- Put personal-pronoun accusatives in their first stage.
- Refresh saved browser queues after the eligibility change.
- Let sparse profiles pass through undersized stages until they can form a
  valid multiple-choice question.

## Constraints

- Preserve generated forms, scores, content imports, and existing verb stages.
- Do not offer locked forms as answer choices.

## Acceptance criteria

- Nominal cards unlock in the documented order after base-word mastery.
- Forms-only entries begin from their first usable stage.
- Existing practised forms remain available and old browser queues refresh.

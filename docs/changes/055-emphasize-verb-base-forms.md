# Emphasize Base Verbs in Form Prompts

## Problem

Verb-form prompts gave equal visual weight to the base verb and the
person-and-blank conjugation cue.

## Desired behavior

The base verb is the primary visual anchor. The person-and-blank cue and tense
label remain clear but secondary.

## Scope

- Adjust only the verb-form prompt hierarchy in the Practice screen.
- Leave nominal and vocabulary prompt layouts unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

- Verb base forms are visibly larger than their conjugation cues.
- Present, past, and conditional cards retain their tense labels.
- The layout remains readable at mobile widths.

# Add Conditional and Essive Cards

## Problem

The generated grammar profiles omit common simple conditional verb forms and
the singular essive nominal case.

## Desired behavior

Generate and stage six affirmative present-conditionals and singular essive
forms without overwhelming a learner or exposing invalid quiz choices.

## Scope

- Add active affirmative, single-word conditional forms only.
- Add singular essive nominal cards.
- Add conditional stages after existing verb stages and essive before nominal
  plural genitive/partitive.
- Label conditional direct prompts and refresh saved queues.

## Constraints

- Exclude negative, passive, and multi-word perfect conditional forms.
- Preserve prior progress and sentence reinforcement behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

- Conditional and essive forms import from Kaikki where applicable.
- New stages require mastery before later stages unlock.
- Sparse profiles still provide enough distinct answer choices.
- Old browser queues refresh and conditional prompts identify their mood.

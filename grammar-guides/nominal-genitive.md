# Genetiivi

## Quick guide

The Finnish genitive usually answers **kenen?** or **minkä?**. It often shows possession or a relationship between two nouns. Many singular genitive forms end in **-n**.

## More detail

Use the genitive to say who owns something or what something belongs to: *talo* becomes *talon* in *talon ovi* — “the house's door” or “the door of the house.”

It is also common before another noun to make a noun phrase. The exact ending changes from word to word, so use the cards to build recognition of the common patterns.

# Partitiivi

## Quick guide

The Finnish partitive often answers **mitä?**. It is used for quantities and substances, incomplete actions, and with many common verbs. Its ending varies by word.

## More detail

In *juon kahvia*, the coffee is an amount rather than one fully bounded item. In *ostan leipää*, the partitive is the object of a common everyday action.

Partitive has many important uses in Finnish, so this guide is only a starting point. For now, connect the form with **mitä?** and notice it in the example sentences.

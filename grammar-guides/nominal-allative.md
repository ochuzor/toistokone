# Allatiivi

## Quick guide

The Finnish allative usually answers **mille?** It is often used for movement onto a surface, direction to a person, or something intended for someone. Many allative forms end in **-lle**.

## More detail

Use the allative when something goes onto an outside surface: *pöytä* becomes *pöydälle* — “onto the table.” It can also show a recipient: *opettaja* becomes *opettajalle* — “to the teacher.”

The ending changes according to the word, so focus first on recognizing the form and connecting it with the question **mille?**. The practice cards will help you learn the common patterns one word at a time.
